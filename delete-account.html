How to Delete Your Account

Method 1 (Inside the App):

  1. Open the আরবি শিখুন লেভেল যাচাই করুন app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile / Account settings and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request to permanently delete your account immediately.

Method 2 (Via Email):

  1. Send an email to informationamq10236@gmail.com from your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - আরবি শিখুন লেভেল যাচাই করুন.
  3. Include your registered email address. Your request will be processed within 7 days.

Data Deleted and Retention Policy

  • Data Deleted: Email address, user ID, account profile information, and saved quiz scores/level progress.
  • Data Retained: None. All associated user data is permanently removed from Firebase once your account is deleted.
